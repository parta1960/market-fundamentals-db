Rank is by the slope m of the least-squares linear fit y = c + m·t (t in years) over the selected window — the metric's change per year in its own units. R² is how much of the variance that straight line explains: green ≥ 0.8 (the slope is a faithful summary), amber 0.5–0.8 (trend with real wobble), grey < 0.5 (the slope is NOT a reliable description — treat with caution). Growth %/yr restates the slope as a percentage of the metric's mean level (blank when the level is too near zero for the ratio to mean anything).
