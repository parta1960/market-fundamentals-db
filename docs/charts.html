🧠 AI Analyst Report —

Valuation ratios pair each quarter's actual close with same-date share counts (no look-ahead). Displayed prices, share counts and per-share values are split-adjusted to today's basis for continuity (Yahoo-comparable). Each chart carries a dotted LINEAR fit over the VISIBLE range, parameters in the corner table: y = c + m·t with t in years (≥6 points, negatives allowed), so c is the level at the start of the range, m the change per year, % the UNITLESS trend (%EPS-style: slope ÷ the latest value — blank when that value is near/below zero) and R² the share of variance the straight line explains — low R² means the trend line is a poor summary of that series. The fit recomputes automatically as new quarters arrive. An amber vertical line marks a STRUCTURAL BREAK — a quarter where revenue stepped to a sustained new level (spin-off, divestiture, deconsolidation or transforming acquisition), detected from the data itself, not a hand-kept list. Before/after such a break the numbers describe different companies, so "fit after break" (on by default) restricts the fits to the period after the most recent one. Negative-earnings quarters show no P/E. Educational tool, not investment advice.
