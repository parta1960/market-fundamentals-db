Valuation ratios pair each quarter's actual close with same-date share counts (no look-ahead). Displayed prices, share counts and per-share values are split-adjusted to today's basis for continuity (Yahoo-comparable). Each chart carries two fits over the VISIBLE range, parameters in the corner table: dashed = exponential y=a·e^(b·t) (least squares on ln(y), positive values only, ≥6 points; b = continuous yearly rate, % = equivalent compound growth per year, R² in log space) and dotted = linear y=c+m·t (all values, R² in plain space). Whichever R² is higher describes the series better. Fits recompute automatically as new quarters arrive. Negative-earnings quarters show no P/E. Educational tool, not investment advice.
