Trend fields come from a least-squares LINEAR fit y = c + m·t (t in years) over the selected window: slope m is the change per year in the series' own units and R² is the share of variance the straight line explains. %EPS, %Rev/sh, %FCF/sh … (v1.16.0) are the UNITLESS versions: the slope divided by the series' LATEST value — e.g. %EPS = EPS slope ($/yr) ÷ current TTM EPS — so companies of any size compare directly. Blank when the latest value is near or below zero (a percentage of ~nothing is noise). All fields, for nine series across three windows, are stored per ticker and screenable by name ("%EPS > 15", "EPS fit R² ≥ 0.9", "revenue per share slope ≥ 1"). Caveats: dual-class tickers (BRK-B, GOOGL, FOX…) can have distorted per-share metrics; very large growth rates are usually M&A, not organic; a small share of underlying fundamentals are reported in non-USD.
